TikTok Repost Remover

Removes all reposts from your profile automatically.

How to get your Session ID:
1. Open tiktok.com on any desktop browser
2. Log in to your account
3. Press F12 → Application → Cookies → tiktok.com
4. Find sessionid and copy the value

Found in browser DevTools → Application → Cookies → tiktok.com
